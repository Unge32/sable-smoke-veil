**SABLE — Smoke & Sapphire** — a top-down heist game. Sable, a cat thief in midnight blue, slips into a Grand Gallery museum at night to steal the Sapphire. Three patrolling guards (Bram, Pip, Odo) with vision cones, two wall cameras, an alarm meter, three routes, and the Sapphire plus three optional trinkets to lift.

The signature is the **Smoke Veil**: throw a smoke bomb and every guard vision cone and camera goes blind for 6 seconds — but the nearest guard comes to investigate the smoke. Sneak, veil, grab, and get out clean.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network, just static files under `game/`.

Controls: **WASD / arrows** move · **Shift** sneak · **Space** smoke veil · **E** decoy · **P** pause · **R** restart.
